Detailed Description

Thin vacuum scoring planes around the UBT.

Every track entering a plane produces one UpstreamTaggerScoringPoint (see SHiP::ScoringPlane for what a point holds), so the flux reaching the decay vessel can be compared with what the UBT sees. The planes and their PDG filters are set with AddPlane, from the geometry configuration.
