Detailed Description

Digitised scoring-plane hit with a particle-identification outcome.

Position and time are smeared from the MC point; the momentum is the MC-truth momentum at the plane. The PID outcome (electron / hadron / muon, or unknown) is drawn with CaloScoringPlanePID from energy-dependent confusion matrices and stored as a CaloScoringPlanePID::Species value. The MC truth link is kept in the separate digiCaloScoringPlane2MC branch.
