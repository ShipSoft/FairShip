Detailed Description

Straw tracker pattern recognition from drift circles.

The spectrometer has two stations before and two after the magnet, whose field (mostly Bx) bends tracks in y. Within each pair of stations tracks are straight lines, which are found from the drift circles of the hits: first in the y views, then, for each y line, in the stereo views. The segments before and after the magnet are then matched, which also gives the momentum and charge.
