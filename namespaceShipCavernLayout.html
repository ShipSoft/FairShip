Detailed Description

Cross-sections of the TCC8 tunnel and the ECN3 hall (cm, beam axis at x = y = 0).

Inner concrete surfaces of the civil-engineering solid in the CAD model ST1967028_01 behind the integration layout SPSXXSHIP0002 version 2026-0.1 (EDMS 3287817 v1.1), sectioned across the beam. The walls drift by 0.35 mrad against the beam; the values are taken at mid-tunnel and mid-hall. Not modelled: the TCC8 arch upstream of z = 6.2 m, the ECN3 crane ledges above y = 5.84 m and the ECN3 arch above the springline.
