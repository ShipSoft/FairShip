Detailed Description

ACTS view of a FairShip magnetic field.

Wraps the field used in the simulation (e.g. the global field of the ShipFieldMaker), so the fit sees the same field map, placement and orientation as Geant4. Positions and field values are converted between the ACTS reco frame and the SHiP frame (cm, kGauss).
