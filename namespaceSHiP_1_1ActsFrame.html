Detailed Description

Conversions between the SHiP frame and the ACTS reconstruction frame.

The SHiP frame has the beam along z and lengths in cm. The ACTS frame used for the fit has the beam along x, so that tracks sit near theta = 90 deg and away from the coordinate singularity of the bound parameters, and lengths in mm:

x_acts = z_ship, y_acts = y_ship, z_acts = -x_ship
