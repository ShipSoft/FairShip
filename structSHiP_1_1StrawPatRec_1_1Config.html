◆ matchSigmaX

double SHiP::StrawPatRec::Config::matchSigmaX = 0.6

cm

Uncertainties of the segment match at the bending plane, as sqrt(a^2 + (b / p)^2) with p in GeV: the straight segments extrapolated to the bending plane deviate more from the track the lower its momentum. From truth-matched segments of 2-100 GeV tracks, with the constant terms widened for busy events, where segments lose hits.
