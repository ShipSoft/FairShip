Detailed Description

Ideal scoring plane downstream of the timing detector.

Replaces the former SplitCal calorimeter. The plane is a thin vacuum box placed where the SplitCal front face used to be. Every track entering the box produces one CaloScoringPlanePoint holding the MC-truth position, momentum, time and PDG code. ProcessHits runs after the step that enters the box, so these are the values at the end of that step, i.e. at the exit face for a particle crossing the plane. No energy deposit is required (the medium is vacuum), so neutral particles are recorded too.

A track crossing the plane twice (e.g. back-scattered) gives two points; the sign of Pz tells the direction.
