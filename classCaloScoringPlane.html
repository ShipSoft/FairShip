Detailed Description

Ideal scoring plane downstream of the timing detector.

Replaces the former SplitCal calorimeter. The plane is a thin vacuum box placed where the SplitCal front face used to be, recording a CaloScoringPlanePoint for every track entering it (see SHiP::ScoringPlane for what a point holds). Its node in the top volume is "CaloScoringPlane_1".
