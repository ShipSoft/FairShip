◆ smoother

Acts::MbfSmoother SHiP::ActsStrawTracker::Impl::smoother

The gain-matrix smoother inverts the predicted covariance, which is nearly singular in the magnet: there, the bending angle is almost fully correlated with the poorly known q/p. Below about 10 GeV/c the smoothed covariance then stops being positive definite. The modified Bryson-Frazier smoother only inverts the residual covariance.
