Detailed Description

template<typename PointType>
class SHiP::ScoringPlane< PointType >

Thin vacuum planes that record every track entering them.

Shared by CaloScoringPlane and UpstreamTaggerScoringPlane, which differ only in their point type (and so their output branch) and detector ID.

Every track entering a plane produces one point holding the MC-truth position, momentum, time and PDG code, with zero energy loss. No energy deposit is required (the medium is vacuum), so neutral particles are recorded too. Position, momentum and time are those at the end of the track's first step in the plane. A track crossing a plane twice (e.g. back-scattered) gives two points; the sign of Pz tells the direction.

The point detector ID is the copy number of the plane: its index + 1, in the order the planes were added. A plane can be restricted to some species, given by their absolute PDG codes.

A point counts towards keeping its track in MCTrack (ShipStack::AddPoint, MCTracksWithHitsOnly) unless the plane is added with keepTracks = false; then the points of tracks that are not kept for another reason get track ID -2, as for other secondaries below the energy cut.
