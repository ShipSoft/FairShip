Detailed Description

Upstream Background Tagger (UBT) detector.

The active plane is split into coarse regions, generated on the UBT box from the rule in geometry/UpstreamTagger_config.yaml (see shipDet_conf.configure_upstream_tagger). Each region records whether it represents 20 x 20 or 40 x 40 mm^2 polystyrene scintillator tiles. Optical photon production and transport are deliberately not simulated here: an MC point stores the energy deposited in a mapped region, identified by its copy number. Only points of tracks entering a region count towards keeping their track in MCTrack, not those of secondaries created in the plastic.
